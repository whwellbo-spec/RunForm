Film yourself running from the side. RunForm tracks your body, scores your stride against the Pose Method and tells you what to drill.

How to film a good clip
  • From the side. Phone on a tripod or ledge at hip height, 3–5 m away, square to your running direction.
  • Slo-mo. Use the Camera app’s Slo-mo mode (240 fps is best).
  • Whole body in frame the entire time, with space above your head and below your feet.
  • Steady pace. On a treadmill, 10–20 s at your usual easy pace. Outdoors, run past the camera a few times.
  • Bright light and fitted clothes so your knees, ankles and feet are easy to see.
  • Save to Files first. In Photos tap Share → Save to Files, then pick it from Files here. Picking straight from Photos can drop frames.
